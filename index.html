    Checking the data
    • OpenStreetMap: building outlines and names, sidewalks and paths, entrances, trees, ground cover, and footways tagged as tunnel, bridge or indoor corridor. Small gaps between mapped sidewalks are closed automatically. Data is bundled and refreshed weekly; Overpass is only queried when no bundle is present.
    • Open-Meteo, live: temperature, wind, cloud cover and precipitation for the chosen date and hour. Cloud cover reduces the sun term; rain adds discomfort wherever there is no cover.
    • Computed: sun position, shadows from building and tree heights, feels-like temperature per segment, comfort-weighted routing, open-hours blocking, turn-by-turn directions.
    • Comfort model: walking time × (1 + slider × stress), where stress follows the UTCI thermal-stress bands of the segment's feels-like temperature. Direct sun adds up to 10 °C at high sun. Indoors counts as 22 °C. Sources in the source comments.
    • Layers tab: every drawn thing can be switched on or off, with counts, so the data can be checked. Doors show tagged (blue) against assumed (amber), crossings are coloured by type, and buildings can be recoloured by where their height came from.
    • Doors: OSM entrances are used with their meaning: main and secondary entrances preferred, service doors penalised, exit-only and entrance-only doors are one-way, emergency exits and private doors are never used, wheelchair=no doors are skipped in step-free mode. Buildings with fewer than two usable tagged entrances get assumed doors where sidewalks meet the wall, flagged in the directions.
    • Safety, always on: these apply even at the Fastest setting. After dark, paths OSM tags lit=yes are preferred and lit=no costs most, with untagged paths in between. Stepping into a street costs seconds-equivalent by what is crossed, from about 4 s for a residential street to 50 s for a primary road, tripled when there is no crossing and raised again at night. At or below freezing, outdoor stairs are treated as slower.
    • Streets: routes never travel along a street carriageway at all: only footways, paths, pedestrian ways, steps, service drives and mapped crossings. Streets are still read, so a footway drawn across one is detected as a crossing and priced by what it crosses. Footways crossing a street with no crossing node are flagged as unmarked and excluded by default.
    • Building codes: the labels are Purdue's official building codes (WALC, PMU, LWSN), matched to the OSM names from the published campus list in data/purdue-building-codes.txt. Run npm run codes after a data refresh; anything unmatched keeps initials from its name.
    • Following you: while your location is live and you are within 40 m of the route, the part you have walked is dropped from the map, the banner moves to the step you are on, and the trip bar counts down the time and distance left. Leave the route by more than 45 m for three fixes in a row and it routes again from where you actually are. Browsers only track while the page is open and the screen is on.
    • Shadows: a caster's shadow is its footprint swept along the sun-opposite direction. Concave footprints are drawn in pieces rather than as a convex hull, so courtyards and notches stay sunlit; the drawn shadow matches the shade the router uses.
    • Basemap: OSM raster tiles by default. Switch to Built-in to draw the map from the bundled extract instead: no tile server, so it keeps working offline.
    • Cutting through buildings: on by default. Switch it off to stay outdoors except for leaving your start and entering your destination.
    • Assumed: height = levels × 3.5 m, else 12 m. Trees 10 m. Canopy over 35 m is discarded as lidar noise. Hours 7 am to 10 pm unless OSM has opening_hours (simple weekday rules are parsed).
    • Tunnels and skywalks: drawn on the map as a dashed line whether or not your route uses one, with a tooltip naming the two buildings. They are shelter rather than shortcuts, so routing only takes one when the weather is actually severe: at or below freezing, in full sun from the mid 80s, or in steady rain. A step through one shows a tunnel arch icon and says which buildings it joins.
    • Not in OSM: Purdue's skywalks and tunnels. Nine tunnel links are traced from Purdue's printed tunnel diagram into campus-overrides.json and marked unverified, so a route using one says so: Stewart–Union, Stewart–Hicks, Union–Union Club, Union–Grant Street Garage, Union–Krannert, Union–Rawls, Krannert–Rawls, Krannert–Hawkins and Krannert–Young. Correct or confirm them after walking them.
