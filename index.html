    Fetching live weather…
    • OpenStreetMap: building outlines and names, sidewalks and paths, entrances, trees, ground cover, and footways tagged as tunnel, bridge or indoor corridor. Small gaps between mapped sidewalks are closed automatically. Data is bundled and refreshed weekly; Overpass is only queried when no bundle is present.
    • Open-Meteo, live: temperature, wind and cloud cover for the chosen date and hour. Cloud cover reduces the sun term.
    • Computed: sun position, shadows from building and tree heights, feels-like temperature per segment, comfort-weighted routing, open-hours blocking, turn-by-turn directions.
    • Comfort model: walking time × (1 + slider × stress), where stress follows the UTCI thermal-stress bands of the segment's feels-like temperature. Direct sun adds up to 10 °C at high sun. Indoors counts as 22 °C. Sources in the source comments.
    • Doors: OSM entrances are used with their meaning: main and secondary entrances preferred, service doors penalised, exit-only and entrance-only doors are one-way, emergency exits and private doors are never used, wheelchair=no doors are skipped in step-free mode. Buildings with fewer than two usable tagged entrances get assumed doors where sidewalks meet the wall, flagged in the directions.
    • Streets: the route never crosses a primary to residential street except on a mapped crossing (signals cost more waiting time than a marked crossing). Footways drawn across a street with no crossing node are flagged as unmarked and excluded by default.
    • Assumed: height = levels × 3.5 m, else 12 m. Trees 10 m. Hours 7 am to 10 pm unless OSM has opening_hours (simple weekday rules are parsed).
    • Not in OSM: most Purdue skywalks and subwalks. Add them to campus-overrides.json after walking them.
